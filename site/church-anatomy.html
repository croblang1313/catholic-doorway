On the Altar

During Mass you will also see these sacred vessels.

Chalice

The cup that holds the wine that becomes the Blood of Christ at Mass.

Paten

The small plate, usually of gold or silver, that holds the large host during Mass.

Ciborium

A covered, cup-shaped vessel that holds the consecrated hosts for Communion and for keeping in the tabernacle.

Monstrance

An ornate stand, often shaped like a sunburst, used to display the Eucharist for adoration.

Good to Know as a Visitor

  • Catholics dip their fingers in holy water and make the Sign of the Cross as they come in, recalling their Baptism. You are welcome to skip it.
  • Before entering a pew, Catholics genuflect (bend the right knee) toward the tabernacle. A bow works too, and visitors may simply sit.
  • If the red sanctuary lamp is lit, Christ is present in the tabernacle, so people keep their voices low.
  • Every church differs: some have the tabernacle in a side chapel, some have the font in its own room. The pieces are the same.
