Examination of conscience

Before Confession, Catholics take a few quiet minutes to look honestly at their lives. Pick the version that fits you, tick anything that applies, and you will have a short list to bring with you.

How to use it

  1. Start with a prayer. Ask the Holy Spirit to help you see yourself truthfully and without fear: “Come, Holy Spirit, show me my sins as you see them, and give me sorrow for them.”
  2. Go through the questions slowly. Think back to your last confession. Tick the ones that apply.
  3. Notice what is serious. A sin is mortal when it is about a grave matter and you did it knowing it was seriously wrong and freely choosing it (Catechism 1857). Mortal sins should be confessed by kind and roughly how many times. Smaller (venial) sins are good to confess too.
  4. Make your list. Tap Show my list at the bottom. You can read it from your phone in the confessional, or write it down.
