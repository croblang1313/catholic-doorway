Diocese of Steubenville

A Catholic diocese based in Steubenville, Ohio, founded in 1944.

Official website

News, Mass times, schools, Catholic Charities, marriage preparation and contacts for every office.

diosteub.org

Cathedral

Holy Name Cathedral, Steubenville. The cathedral is the bishop’s own church and the mother church of the diocese. See it on a map.

Thinking of becoming Catholic?

OCIA happens in parishes. Pick a parish near you, then call or email and ask when the next OCIA group starts. The diocese’s Office of Worship (sometimes called Evangelization or Christian Initiation) can help if a parish doesn’t answer. How OCIA works.
