Archdiocese of Philadelphia

A Catholic archdiocese based in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, founded in 1875.

Who leads it

Nelson J. Perez, since January 2020.

From Wikidata. If a new archbishop was named recently, the archdiocese website will have it first.

Official website

News, Mass times, schools, Catholic Charities, marriage preparation and contacts for every office.

archphila.org

Cathedral

Cathedral Basilica of Saints Peter and Paul, Philadelphia. The cathedral is the archbishop’s own church and the mother church of the archdiocese. See it on a map.

Thinking of becoming Catholic?

OCIA happens in parishes. Pick a parish near you, then call or email and ask when the next OCIA group starts. The archdiocese’s Office of Worship (sometimes called Evangelization or Christian Initiation) can help if a parish doesn’t answer. How OCIA works.

Find parishes near Philadelphia Search its website for OCIA

Size

About 1,437,400 Catholics, according to the most recent figure in Wikidata.

Holy men and women of Pennsylvania

Saints, Blesseds, Venerables and Servants of God who were born or died in Pennsylvania. Some causes are led by this diocese; others by a neighboring diocese or a religious order.

  • John Neumann Saint (1811–1860)
  • Katharine Drexel Saint (1858–1955)
  • Cornelia Connelly Venerable (1809–1879)
  • Demetrius Augustine Gallitzin Servant of God (1770–1840)
  • John Hardon Servant of God (1914–2000)
