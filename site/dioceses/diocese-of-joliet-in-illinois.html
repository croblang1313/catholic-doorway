Diocese of Joliet in Illinois

A Catholic diocese based in Crest Hill, Illinois, founded in 1948.

Who leads it

Brendan Cahill, since September 2026.

From Wikidata. If a new bishop was named recently, the diocese website will have it first.

Official website

News, Mass times, schools, Catholic Charities, marriage preparation and contacts for every office.

diojoliet.org

Cathedral

Cathedral of St. Raymond Nonnatus, Crest Hill. The cathedral is the bishop’s own church and the mother church of the diocese. See it on a map.

Thinking of becoming Catholic?

OCIA happens in parishes. Pick a parish near you, then call or email and ask when the next OCIA group starts. The diocese’s Office of Worship (sometimes called Evangelization or Christian Initiation) can help if a parish doesn’t answer. How OCIA works.

Find parishes near Crest Hill Search its website for OCIA

Size

About 628,230 Catholics, according to the most recent figure in Wikidata.

Holy men and women of Illinois

Saints, Blesseds, Venerables and Servants of God who were born or died in Illinois. Some causes are led by this diocese; others by a neighboring diocese or a religious order.

  • Frances Xavier Cabrini Saint (1850–1917)
  • Fulton J. Sheen Blessed (1895–1979)
  • Augustine Tolton Venerable (1854–1897)
  • Mary Theresa Dudzik Venerable (1860–1918)
