Tips for parents

  • Start small. One prayer at bedtime, said together every night, does more than a long prayer said once.
  • Let them see you pray. Children learn the faith first by watching the grown-ups they love.
  • Bring them to Mass. Wiggles are normal. Sit near the front so they can see, and whisper what is happening.
  • Make it part of the year. An Advent wreath, a Lent jar or a saint’s day dessert turns the Church calendar into family memories.
  • Ask your parish. Most parishes have religious education classes and sacrament preparation for children.

Getting ready for a sacrament? See First Communion, Confirmation, the examination of conscience for kids and the printable prep sheets in the Family Corner.
