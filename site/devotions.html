Catholic devotions

Beyond the Mass, Catholics have many simple ways to pray through the day and the year. Here are the best-loved, with the words to pray and reminders for the right days and times.

Coming up

    The Rosary

    Twenty minutes with Mary, walking through the life of Jesus. Pray it bead by bead.

    Pray the Rosary →

    Divine Mercy Chaplet

    A ten-minute prayer on rosary beads for mercy on the whole world, often prayed at 3 p.m.

    Pray the chaplet →

    Stations of the Cross

    Walk with Jesus to Calvary in fourteen stations, especially on the Fridays of Lent.

    Pray the Stations →

    Novenas

    Nine days of prayer before a feast: St. Joseph, the Holy Spirit, Divine Mercy, Guadalupe and more.

    See this year’s novenas →

    The Angelus

    A one-minute prayer at 6 a.m., noon and 6 p.m. remembering the Incarnation.

    Pray the Angelus →

    Liturgy of the Hours

    The Church’s own daily prayer of psalms, morning and evening.

    Pray today’s Hours →

    Examination of conscience

    A quiet look back before Confession, or at the end of each day.

    Examine your conscience →

    Reminders

    Get prayer reminders on your phone

    Choose what you would like, then add it to your calendar. Reminders arrive at your own local time, and your calendar keeps the novena dates up to date every year. Nothing to sign up for.

    Reminders to include
    iPhone / Apple Calendar Google Calendar Outlook

    On Android, use Google Calendar. If your calendar app does not show an alert, open the event’s settings and turn on notifications for this calendar. Prefer just the holy days? See the Holy Days Calendar.
