Prayer Wall

Ask for prayer, and pray for others. Share an intention with a first name or anonymously, and tap I prayed when you have prayed for someone. No account, no sign-up, nothing to buy.

In crisis or thinking about suicide?

You don’t have to carry this alone, and you don’t have to wait. In the U.S., call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) any time, day or night. If someone is in immediate danger, call 911.

Call 988 Text 988 Chat online

Outside the U.S., find a free helpline at findahelpline.com. A priest can also visit or talk with you: call any Catholic parish.

Pray for others

Each request was sent by a visitor and read by our small team before it appeared. Requests stay for 30 days. Pray in your own words, or with a Hail Mary, then tap I prayed so the person knows they are not alone.

Ask for prayer

Tell us what you would like prayer for. Our team prays for every request we receive, including private ones.

How we handle what you send: Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

Before you send

  • Use first names only. Please leave out last names, phone numbers, addresses and private details about other people.
  • Every request is read before it appears, usually within a day. We may shorten a request, or keep it private, to protect you or someone else.
  • No links, ads or fundraising. Requests are removed automatically after 30 days.
  • Under 13? Ask a parent before you post.

A prayer for everyone on this wall

Pray this for all who have asked for prayer here, by name if you like.

Lord Jesus, you told us to ask, to seek and to knock. We bring before you everyone who has asked for prayer here, and every need they carry in their hearts. Heal the sick, comfort those who grieve, give work to those who need it, peace to the anxious and hope to those who feel alone. Holy Mary, Mother of God, and all the saints, pray for them. Amen.

More ways to ask for prayer

Have a Mass offered

Call your parish office and ask to have a Mass said for your intention, for someone who is sick or for someone who has died. A small offering is customary but never required.

Ask your parish

Many parishes have a prayer chain or a book of intentions near the door. Priests and deacons are glad to pray with you in person.

Pray the Rosary for someone

Offer a Rosary for a person on this wall. Each decade can be for a different intention.
