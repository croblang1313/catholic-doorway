Dioceses

Find Your Diocese

Every parish belongs to a diocese, a local church led by a bishop. Your diocese can help with OCIA, marriage preparation, Catholic schools and much more.

Which diocese am I in?

Dioceses usually follow county lines. The quickest way to find yours is to find your nearest parish. Its website will name its diocese.

Find a parish Full list at USCCB

What a diocese can help with

  • Finding an active OCIA group
  • Marriage preparation and annulment questions
  • Catholic schools and youth ministry
  • Catholic Charities and social services
  • Safe environment and abuse reporting

Other U.S. jurisdictions

Military Services

The Archdiocese for the Military Services, USA serves Catholics in the armed forces, veterans’ hospitals and government service overseas.

milarch.org

The Personal Ordinariate

The Personal Ordinariate of the Chair of St. Peter welcomes former Anglicans and Episcopalians into the Catholic Church while keeping elements of their heritage.

ordinariate.net

Eastern Catholic Churches

Eastern Catholics in the U.S. have their own eparchies, including the Ukrainian Catholic Archeparchy of Philadelphia and the Byzantine Catholic Archeparchy of Pittsburgh, plus Maronite, Melkite, Chaldean, Syro-Malabar and others.

See all eparchies at USCCB
