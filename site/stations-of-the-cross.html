The Stations of the Cross

For centuries Christians have walked in prayer with Jesus on his way to Calvary. Every Catholic church has fourteen small images or crosses along its walls for this. You can pray the Stations there, at home, or anywhere, in about 20 minutes.

Pray it now

One station at a time

Opens each station in large print with Back and Next buttons, so you can walk from station to station in church with your phone. Your screen stays on and your place is saved.

When: any time, but especially on the Fridays of Lent and on Good Friday, when most parishes pray them together, often on Friday evening. Get a reminder on Fridays in Lent.

Before the first station

Make the Sign of the Cross. Many people pray the Act of Contrition here, then begin.

Leader
We adore you, O Christ, and we praise you.
All
Because by your holy cross you have redeemed the world.

At each station, genuflect or bow if you can, and say these two lines. After the reflection, pray an Our Father, a Hail Mary and a Glory Be.

1. Jesus is condemned to death

Mark 15:1–15

Pilate knows Jesus is innocent, but he is afraid of the crowd and hands him over. Jesus says nothing in his own defense.

Leader
We adore you, O Christ, and we praise you.
All
Because by your holy cross you have redeemed the world.

Lord, when it is easier to go along with the crowd, give me the courage to stand with you.

2. Jesus takes up his cross

John 19:16–17

The heavy beam is laid on shoulders already torn by the scourging. Jesus accepts it, for us.

Leader
We adore you, O Christ, and we praise you.
All
Because by your holy cross you have redeemed the world.

Lord, help me carry my own crosses with patience, and not to add to the crosses of others.

3. Jesus falls the first time

Isaiah 53:4–6

Weak from pain and loss of blood, Jesus falls. He gets up and keeps going.

Leader
We adore you, O Christ, and we praise you.
All
Because by your holy cross you have redeemed the world.

Lord, when I fall into the same sins again, help me to get up and begin again with you.

4. Jesus meets his mother

Luke 2:34–35

Mary sees her son on the road to death. She cannot take his pain away, but she does not leave him.

Leader
We adore you, O Christ, and we praise you.
All
Because by your holy cross you have redeemed the world.

Mary, stay close to me and to everyone who suffers, as you stayed close to your son.

5. Simon of Cyrene helps Jesus carry the cross

Mark 15:21

Simon is pulled from the crowd and made to help. He did not choose this, but he will always be remembered for it.

Leader
We adore you, O Christ, and we praise you.
All
Because by your holy cross you have redeemed the world.

Lord, open my eyes to the people around me who need a hand with their burden.

6. Veronica wipes the face of Jesus

Isaiah 52:14; 53:2–3

Tradition says a woman pushed through the soldiers to wipe the blood and sweat from his face. A small act of love, at real risk.

Leader
We adore you, O Christ, and we praise you.
All
Because by your holy cross you have redeemed the world.

Lord, let me see your face in the poor, the sick and the forgotten, and not look away.

7. Jesus falls the second time

Psalm 22:6–7

He falls again, and the crowd mocks him. Still he rises.

Leader
We adore you, O Christ, and we praise you.
All
Because by your holy cross you have redeemed the world.

Lord, when I am discouraged and tempted to give up, lift me up.

8. Jesus meets the women of Jerusalem

Luke 23:27–31

Women weep for him along the road. Even in agony, Jesus turns to comfort and teach them.

Leader
We adore you, O Christ, and we praise you.
All
Because by your holy cross you have redeemed the world.

Lord, teach me to think of others even when I am hurting.

9. Jesus falls the third time

Lamentations 3:1–16

Near the top of the hill, his strength is gone and he falls once more. He gets up to finish what he came to do.

Leader
We adore you, O Christ, and we praise you.
All
Because by your holy cross you have redeemed the world.

Lord, give me the strength to persevere to the end.

10. Jesus is stripped of his garments

John 19:23–24

The soldiers take his clothes and gamble for them. He is left with nothing, not even his dignity in the eyes of the world.

Leader
We adore you, O Christ, and we praise you.
All
Because by your holy cross you have redeemed the world.

Lord, free me from clinging to things, and help me respect the dignity of every person.

11. Jesus is nailed to the cross

Luke 23:33–34

They drive nails through his hands and feet. He prays, “Father, forgive them; for they know not what they do.”

Leader
We adore you, O Christ, and we praise you.
All
Because by your holy cross you have redeemed the world.

Lord, help me forgive the people who have hurt me, as you forgave.

12. Jesus dies on the cross

John 19:25–30

After three hours, Jesus says, “It is finished,” bows his head and gives up his spirit. Kneel here for a moment of silence.

Leader
We adore you, O Christ, and we praise you.
All
Because by your holy cross you have redeemed the world.

Lord Jesus, you died so that I might live. Thank you. Let me never forget it.

13. Jesus is taken down from the cross

John 19:38

Joseph of Arimathea and Nicodemus lower his body and place it in the arms of his mother.

Leader
We adore you, O Christ, and we praise you.
All
Because by your holy cross you have redeemed the world.

Mary, Mother of Sorrows, pray for all who grieve.

14. Jesus is laid in the tomb

John 19:39–42

His body is wrapped in linen with spices and laid in a new tomb, and a stone is rolled across the door. The world waits in silence for Easter.

Leader
We adore you, O Christ, and we praise you.
All
Because by your holy cross you have redeemed the world.

Lord, when it is dark and you seem far away, help me wait in hope for the morning.

After the fourteenth station

Many people end with an Our Father, a Hail Mary and a Glory Be for the intentions of the Pope, and with a short prayer of thanks: Lord Jesus, by your cross and resurrection you have set us free. Help me to follow you each day. Amen.

Some parishes add a fifteenth moment for the Resurrection, since the story does not end at the tomb.

“I am the door; if any one enters by me, he will be saved.”

John 10:9 (RSV-CE)
