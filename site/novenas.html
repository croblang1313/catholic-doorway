Novenas

A novena is nine days of prayer for a special intention, usually leading up to a feast. The first novena was the nine days Mary and the apostles prayed together between the Ascension and Pentecost.

How to pray a novena

  1. Choose an intention. Something you need, someone you love, or simply to grow closer to God.
  2. Pray each day for nine days in a row. Pray the novena prayer below, then an Our Father, a Hail Mary and a Glory Be. Five minutes is enough.
  3. If you miss a day, just pick up where you left off. A novena is a habit of trust, not a test.

The dates below are worked out for this year. Get a reminder each morning of every novena.

Novenas through the year

Novena to St. Joseph

Ends on the Solemnity of St. Joseph, March 19.

Prayer for each day

St. Joseph, guardian of Jesus and faithful husband of Mary, you worked with your hands, protected your family and trusted God in the dark. Watch over my family and my work. Help me with this need: (name it). Teach me to listen for God’s voice as you did, and to do what he asks without delay. Amen.

Divine Mercy Novena

Begins on Good Friday and ends the day before Divine Mercy Sunday. Pray the Divine Mercy Chaplet each day for that day’s intention.

Intentions for each day

  1. Day 1. All people, especially sinners
  2. Day 2. Priests and religious
  3. Day 3. All devout and faithful souls
  4. Day 4. Those who do not believe in God and those who do not yet know Jesus
  5. Day 5. Christians separated from the Church
  6. Day 6. The meek and humble, and children
  7. Day 7. Those who especially honor God’s mercy
  8. Day 8. The souls in purgatory
  9. Day 9. Those who have grown lukewarm in faith

Prayer for each day

Jesus, I trust in you. Today I bring before your merciful Heart those you asked us to pray for on this day of the novena. Have mercy on them and on the whole world. Amen.

Novena to the Holy Spirit

The first novena: the apostles prayed with Mary for nine days between the Ascension and Pentecost (Acts 1:14). It ends the day before Pentecost.

Prayer for each day

Come, Holy Spirit, fill my heart and kindle in me the fire of your love. Give me wisdom to see what matters, courage to do what is right, and peace in what I cannot change. Renew your Church and renew the face of the earth. Amen.

Novena to the Sacred Heart of Jesus

Ends the day before the Solemnity of the Sacred Heart, the Friday after Corpus Christi.

Prayer for each day

Most Sacred Heart of Jesus, you have loved me without limit. I place my trust in you and bring you this intention: (name it). Make my heart more like yours: gentle, humble and generous. Amen.

Novena to St. Anthony of Padua

Ends the day before his feast on June 13. Many people pray to St. Anthony for lost things, and for the poor.

Prayer for each day

St. Anthony, friend of the poor and finder of what is lost, you preached the Gospel with joy and loved the Lord with all your heart. Help me find what I have lost, above all any faith or hope I have let slip away. Please pray for this intention: (name it). Amen.

Novena for the Assumption

Ends the day before the Assumption of Mary on August 15.

Prayer for each day

Mary, taken up body and soul into heaven, you show us where our journey ends. Help me keep my eyes on heaven while I walk on earth, and pray for me now and at the hour of my death. Amen.

Novena to St. Thérèse of Lisieux

Ends the day before her feast on October 1. St. Thérèse promised to spend her heaven doing good on earth.

Prayer for each day

St. Thérèse, Little Flower, you found the way to holiness in small things done with great love. Teach me your little way of trust. Please ask God for this intention: (name it). Amen.

Novena to St. Jude

Ends the day before the feast of Sts. Simon and Jude on October 28. St. Jude is the patron of difficult and desperate cases.

Prayer for each day

St. Jude, apostle and friend of Jesus, people turn to you when things seem hopeless. I bring you this need: (name it). Pray that God will help me, and give me the grace to accept his will with peace. Amen.

Novena to the Immaculate Conception

Ends the day before the Immaculate Conception on December 8, the patronal feast of the United States.

Prayer for each day

Mary, conceived without sin, God prepared you from the first moment of your life to be the mother of his Son. Pray for me, that I may turn away from sin and say yes to God as you did. Watch over our country and all its people. Amen.

Novena to Our Lady of Guadalupe

Ends the day before her feast on December 12. She appeared to St. Juan Diego in 1531 and is patroness of the Americas.

Prayer for each day

Our Lady of Guadalupe, you told St. Juan Diego, “Am I not here, I who am your mother?” Hold me and my family close. Pray for the unborn, for migrants and for everyone far from home. I bring you this intention: (name it). Amen.

Christmas Novena

The nine days before Christmas, December 16 to 24. In Spanish-speaking parishes these evenings are kept as the Posadas.

Prayer for each day

Come, Lord Jesus. As Mary and Joseph searched for a place to stay, make room in my heart for you. Prepare my family to welcome you this Christmas, and let me bring your peace to someone who needs it. Amen.
