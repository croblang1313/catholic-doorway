The Catechism and Church documents

Where to find what the Church actually teaches, in her own words. Look up any paragraph of the Catechism, browse it by topic, or search the major documents of the Popes and the Second Vatican Council. Every link opens the official text on the Vatican’s website.

The Catechism of the Catholic Church

Published by St. John Paul II in 1992, the Catechism is the full summary of Catholic belief in 2,865 numbered paragraphs. When people cite “CCC 1324,” they mean paragraph 1324. It has four parts: what we believe (the Creed), how we worship (the sacraments), how we live (the moral life and the Ten Commandments), and how we pray (the Our Father).

New to the Catechism?

Start with the Compendium, the Catechism in short questions and answers, or with our plain-language What Catholics Believe and Honest Questions. For teens, many parishes use YOUCAT, a youth catechism. In the U.S., the bishops also publish the United States Catholic Catechism for Adults.

Major Church documents

The Popes teach through encyclicals (letters to the whole Church), apostolic exhortations (often after a meeting of bishops called a synod) and apostolic letters. The Second Vatican Council (1962–1965) gathered the world’s bishops and produced sixteen documents that shape Catholic life today. Documents are usually known by their first words in Latin, such as Lumen Gentium, “Light of the Nations.”

  • Catechism · 1992Catechism of the Catholic Church

    The full, official summary of what the Church believes, how she worships, how Catholics live and how they pray, in 2,865 numbered paragraphs.

    St. John Paul II · Faith and God, Sacraments, Moral life, Prayer and holiness

  • Catechism · 2005Compendium of the Catechism

    The Catechism in 598 short questions and answers. A good place to start.

    Benedict XVI · Faith and God, Sacraments, Moral life, Prayer and holiness

  • Church law · 1983Codex Iuris CanoniciCode of Canon Law

    The Church’s law for the Latin Church: the sacraments, marriage, parishes, clergy and the rights and duties of the faithful.

    St. John Paul II · The Church, Sacraments

  • Vatican II · 1963Sacrosanctum ConciliumConstitution on the Sacred Liturgy

    Why and how the Church worships, and the reform of the Mass and sacraments, including the use of local languages.

    Second Vatican Council · Mass and liturgy, Sacraments

  • Vatican II · 1964Lumen GentiumDogmatic Constitution on the Church

    What the Church is: the People of God, bishops and the Pope, the universal call to holiness, and Mary’s place in the Church.

    Second Vatican Council · The Church, Mary

  • Vatican II · 1965Dei VerbumDogmatic Constitution on Divine Revelation

    How God speaks to us through Scripture and Tradition, and why every Catholic should read the Bible.

    Second Vatican Council · Scripture, Faith and God

  • Vatican II · 1965Gaudium et SpesPastoral Constitution on the Church in the Modern World

    The Church and today’s world: human dignity, marriage and family, culture, economic life, politics, war and peace.

    Second Vatican Council · Social teaching, Marriage and family, Human life

  • Vatican II · 1964Unitatis RedintegratioDecree on Ecumenism

    How Catholics should work and pray for unity with other Christians.

    Second Vatican Council · Other Christians and religions

  • Vatican II · 1964Orientalium EcclesiarumDecree on the Eastern Catholic Churches

    The dignity and traditions of the Eastern Catholic Churches, such as the Byzantine, Maronite and Chaldean.

    Second Vatican Council · The Church

  • Vatican II · 1963Inter MirificaDecree on the Media of Social Communications

    The press, film, radio and television, and how to use the media for good.

    Second Vatican Council · Social teaching, Mission and evangelization

  • Vatican II · 1965Christus DominusDecree on the Pastoral Office of Bishops

    The work of bishops in their dioceses and together with the Pope.

    Second Vatican Council · The Church

  • Vatican II · 1965Perfectae CaritatisDecree on the Renewal of Religious Life

    Renewal for religious sisters, brothers and priests in their communities.

    Second Vatican Council · The Church

  • Vatican II · 1965Optatam TotiusDecree on the Training of Priests

    How seminarians should be formed in prayer, study and pastoral life.

    Second Vatican Council · The Church

  • Vatican II · 1965Apostolicam ActuositatemDecree on the Apostolate of the Laity

    Every baptized person shares in the Church’s mission, at home, at work and in society.

    Second Vatican Council · Mission and evangelization, The Church

  • Vatican II · 1965Ad GentesDecree on the Mission Activity of the Church

    The Church is missionary by nature: bringing the Gospel to every people.

    Second Vatican Council · Mission and evangelization

  • Vatican II · 1965Presbyterorum OrdinisDecree on the Ministry and Life of Priests

    Who priests are, what they do, and how they should live.

    Second Vatican Council · The Church, Sacraments

  • Vatican II · 1965Gravissimum EducationisDeclaration on Christian Education

    The right to education, the role of parents, and Catholic schools and universities.

    Second Vatican Council · Marriage and family, Social teaching

  • Vatican II · 1965Nostra AetateDeclaration on Non-Christian Religions

    The Church’s respect for other religions, especially the Jewish people and Muslims, and its rejection of antisemitism.

    Second Vatican Council · Other Christians and religions

  • Vatican II · 1965Dignitatis HumanaeDeclaration on Religious Freedom

    Every person has the right to seek the truth about God freely, without coercion by the state.

    Second Vatican Council · Social teaching, Faith and God

  • Encyclical · 1891Rerum NovarumOn Capital and Labor

    The first great social encyclical: the rights of workers, fair wages, unions and private property.

    Leo XIII · Social teaching

  • Encyclical · 1931Quadragesimo AnnoOn Reconstruction of the Social Order

    Forty years after Rerum Novarum: social justice and the principle of subsidiarity.

    Pius XI · Social teaching

  • Encyclical · 1943Mystici Corporis ChristiOn the Mystical Body of Christ

    The Church as the Body of Christ, with Christ as its head.

    Pius XII · The Church

  • Encyclical · 1963Pacem in TerrisPeace on Earth

    Human rights and duties as the foundation of peace among nations, written during the Cold War.

    St. John XXIII · Social teaching

  • Encyclical · 1968Humanae VitaeOn Human Life

    Married love and the transmission of life, and why the Church teaches against artificial contraception.

    St. Paul VI · Marriage and family, Human life, Moral life

  • Encyclical · 1979Redemptor HominisThe Redeemer of Man

    St. John Paul II’s first encyclical: Christ reveals to every person who he or she truly is.

    St. John Paul II · Faith and God

  • Encyclical · 1981Laborem ExercensOn Human Work

    The dignity of work and of the worker, who always matters more than profit.

    St. John Paul II · Social teaching

  • Encyclical · 1993Veritatis SplendorThe Splendor of Truth

    The foundations of Catholic moral teaching: freedom, conscience, and acts that are always wrong.

    St. John Paul II · Moral life

  • Encyclical · 1995Evangelium VitaeThe Gospel of Life

    The value of every human life: abortion, euthanasia and the death penalty.

    St. John Paul II · Human life, Moral life

  • Encyclical · 1995Ut Unum SintThat They May Be One

    The Catholic commitment to Christian unity.

    St. John Paul II · Other Christians and religions

  • Encyclical · 1998Fides et RatioFaith and Reason

    Faith and reason are “like two wings” that lift the human spirit toward the truth.

    St. John Paul II · Faith and God

  • Encyclical · 2003Ecclesia de EucharistiaOn the Eucharist

    The Church draws her life from the Eucharist, the real presence of Christ.

    St. John Paul II · Sacraments, Mass and liturgy

  • Encyclical · 2005Deus Caritas EstGod Is Love

    God’s love for us, and the Church’s works of charity that flow from it.

    Benedict XVI · Faith and God, Social teaching

  • Encyclical · 2007Spe SalviSaved in Hope

    Christian hope, judgment and eternal life, and places to learn hope, like prayer and suffering.

    Benedict XVI · Faith and God, Prayer and holiness

  • Encyclical · 2009Caritas in VeritateCharity in Truth

    Human development, the global economy and the common good after the 2008 financial crisis.

    Benedict XVI · Social teaching

  • Encyclical · 2013Lumen FideiThe Light of Faith

    Faith as a light for the whole of life, begun by Benedict XVI and finished by Francis.

    Francis · Faith and God

  • Encyclical · 2015Laudato Si’On Care for Our Common Home

    The environment, the poor and “integral ecology”: everything is connected.

    Francis · Social teaching

  • Encyclical · 2020Fratelli TuttiOn Fraternity and Social Friendship

    Building a world of brothers and sisters, through the parable of the Good Samaritan.

    Francis · Social teaching

  • Encyclical · 2024Dilexit NosHe Loved Us

    On the human and divine love of the Heart of Jesus Christ.

    Francis · Faith and God, Prayer and holiness

  • Encyclical · 2026Magnifica HumanitasOn Safeguarding the Human Person in the Time of Artificial Intelligence

    Pope Leo XIV’s first encyclical: the Church’s social teaching applied to AI, work, truth, freedom and war.

    Leo XIV · Social teaching, Human life

  • Apostolic exhortation · 1975Evangelii NuntiandiOn Evangelization in the Modern World

    The Church exists to evangelize; people today listen to witnesses more than to teachers.

    St. Paul VI · Mission and evangelization

  • Apostolic exhortation · 1981Familiaris ConsortioOn the Christian Family

    The family as a community of love and life, and the “domestic church.”

    St. John Paul II · Marriage and family

  • Apostolic exhortation · 2007Sacramentum CaritatisOn the Eucharist

    The Eucharist believed, celebrated and lived.

    Benedict XVI · Sacraments, Mass and liturgy

  • Apostolic exhortation · 2010Verbum DominiThe Word of the Lord

    The Bible in the life of the Church, including lectio divina, praying with Scripture.

    Benedict XVI · Scripture

  • Apostolic exhortation · 2013Evangelii GaudiumThe Joy of the Gospel

    A call to every Catholic to share the Gospel with joy, and a Church that goes out to the margins.

    Francis · Mission and evangelization, Social teaching

  • Apostolic exhortation · 2016Amoris LaetitiaThe Joy of Love

    Love in the family, marriage preparation, raising children and accompanying families in difficulty.

    Francis · Marriage and family

  • Apostolic exhortation · 2018Gaudete et ExsultateRejoice and Be Glad

    The call to holiness in today’s world, for ordinary people: the “saints next door.”

    Francis · Prayer and holiness, Moral life

  • Apostolic exhortation · 2019Christus VivitChrist Is Alive

    A letter to young people and to everyone who works with them.

    Francis · Mission and evangelization, Marriage and family

  • Apostolic exhortation · 2025Dilexi TeI Have Loved You

    Pope Leo XIV on love for the poor, at the heart of the Gospel and of the Church’s history.

    Leo XIV · Social teaching

  • Apostolic letter · 1984Salvifici DolorisOn the Christian Meaning of Human Suffering

    How suffering, joined to Christ’s, can have meaning and bear fruit.

    St. John Paul II · Faith and God, Human life

    The equal dignity of women and men, and the “genius” of women, with Mary as the model.

  • Apostolic letter · 1994Ordinatio SacerdotalisOn Reserving Priestly Ordination to Men Alone

  • Apostolic letter · 1998Dies DominiOn Keeping the Lord’s Day Holy
