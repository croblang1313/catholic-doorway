About CatholicDoorway

A free, ad-free guide to the Catholic faith in one simple place.

Why this site exists

Good Catholic resources are spread across many websites. CatholicDoorway brings the essentials together for two kinds of people: Catholics who want a quick, reliable reference, and anyone who is curious about the faith or thinking about becoming Catholic.

Our name comes from Jesus’ words in John 10:9: “I am the door; if any one enters by me, he will be saved.”

CatholicDoorway is an independent project. It is not an official website of the Catholic Church, the USCCB or any diocese. For official teaching, we point you to the Catechism of the Catholic Church, the U.S. bishops and the Holy See.

Sources and data

  • Scripture on this site is quoted from the Revised Standard Version, Second Catholic Edition. Daily readings link to the USCCB, which publishes the translation used at Mass in the U.S.
  • Prayers are given in their traditional English wording.
  • Church locations come from OpenStreetMap, © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License. Anyone can improve this data, and fixes you make on OpenStreetMap appear on our map.
  • Mass times change often. Always confirm them with the parish itself.

Privacy

We don’t use ads, trackers or accounts. If you use “Near me” on the map, your browser shares your approximate location only to search for nearby churches. We never store it.

Corrections and suggestions

Spotted a mistake, a missing church or a term we should add? We’d be grateful to hear from you. Email hello@catholicdoorway.com.

For a church’s address, phone or website, the fastest fix is to edit it on OpenStreetMap. Each listing on our map has an “Improve this listing” link.
