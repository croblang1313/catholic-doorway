The Mass for Kids

What happens at Mass, step by step, in words a child can follow: when to stand, sit and kneel, what we say, and what to watch for.

What happens at Mass

Mass has many parts. These are the ones children can watch for. The words in each step are what the people say.

  1. 1

    Sign of the Cross and greeting

    Stand

    We begin the way we begin every prayer. Make the Sign of the Cross with the priest: forehead, chest, left shoulder, right shoulder.

    Priest
    In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit.
    All
    Amen.

  2. 2

    Asking for mercy

    Stand

    We tell God we are sorry for the times we did not love Him and other people, and we ask Him to forgive us.

    Priest or cantor
    Lord, have mercy.
    All
    Lord, have mercy.

  3. 3

    Glory to God

    Stand

    On most Sundays we sing a happy song of praise, like the angels sang when Jesus was born. (Not in Advent or Lent.)

  4. 4

    First reading

    Sit

    Time to sit and listen. God talks to us through the Bible. Listen for one word or name you can remember.

    Reader
    The word of the Lord.
    All
    Thanks be to God.

  5. 5

    The Gospel

    Stand

    We stand up because now we hear about Jesus Himself. Trace a little cross on your forehead, your lips and your heart: God’s word in my mind, on my lips and in my heart.

    Priest
    The Lord be with you.
    All
    And with your spirit.

  6. 6

    Homily

    Sit

    The priest or deacon helps us understand the readings. Try to find one thing he says that you can do this week.

  7. 7

    Preparing the gifts

    Sit

    People carry bread and wine to the altar. You can give God a gift too: something good you did this week, or something that makes you sad or happy.

  8. 8

    Holy, Holy, Holy

    Stand

    We sing “Holy, Holy, Holy” together with all the angels and saints in heaven.

  9. 9

    Eucharistic Prayer

    Kneel

    This is the most important part, so we kneel. The priest says Jesus’ own words, and the bread and wine become Jesus’ Body and Blood. Listen for the bells, look at the Host when the priest lifts it up, and whisper, “My Lord and my God.”

  10. 10

    The Lord’s Prayer

    Stand

    We pray the prayer Jesus taught us, all together, as one big family of God.

    All
    Our Father, who art in heaven… but deliver us from evil.
    All
    For the kingdom, the power and the glory are yours, now and for ever.

  11. 11

    Sign of peace

    Stand

    We share Jesus’ peace with the people near us. Shake hands, wave or smile.

    You
    Peace be with you.

  12. 12

    Holy Communion

    As you wish

    People who have made their First Communion receive Jesus. If you have not made yours yet, you can come up with your family and cross your arms over your chest; in many parishes the priest will give you a blessing.

    Minister
    The Body of Christ.
    Catholic receiving
    Amen.

  13. 13

    Blessing and dismissal

    Stand

    The priest blesses us and sends us out to love and serve the Lord. We take Jesus with us all week long.

    Priest
    The Lord be with you.
    All
    And with your spirit.

Tips for Mass with children

  • Sit near the front, where children can see what is happening.
  • Look at the readings together before Mass, or read them on the way.
  • Whisper what is coming next: “Now the bells will ring.”
  • Bring a small children’s missal or picture book about the Mass.
  • If your child needs a break, step out and come back. Everyone has been there.

Grown-ups can learn more in Inside the Mass and Your First Mass. Kids can color the altar and God’s Word at Mass.
