Privacy Policy

CatholicDoorway has no ads, no advertising trackers and no accounts. This page explains, in plain words, the little information the site does handle.

Effective November 21, 2026

The short version

  • You can read every page without signing up or telling us who you are.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade personal information, and we use no advertising or tracking cookies. We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies and does not identify you.
  • The only personal information we keep is what you choose to send us: an email, a parish OCIA listing, or a prayer request.

Information you send us

Parish OCIA listing form

The List your parish’s OCIA form asks for the parish name, city, state and diocese; a contact name, role, email and phone; the parish website; when OCIA meets, how to start, the Mass and Confession times, the languages offered and any notes. We also record when the form was sent and in which language.

Submissions are stored with our host, Cloudflare, and stay private until we have checked them with the parish. Once a listing is approved, those details (but not the submission time) are shown publicly on this site so that people looking for OCIA know whom to contact. That is the whole purpose of the form, so please send only contact details the parish has agreed to publish. We delete submissions we decide not to publish, and we remove any listing when the parish asks.

Prayer Wall

The Prayer Wall asks for an optional first name and your prayer request. We also record when you sent it, the language of the page and whether you asked to keep it private. Please do not include last names, contact details or private details about other people; we may remove them before publishing.

Requests are stored with Cloudflare. A public request is read by our team and, once approved, shown on the Prayer Wall with your first name (or “Anonymous”), how long ago it was sent and how many people have prayed. A private request is read only by our team and is never published. Every request, public or private, is deleted automatically 30 days after you send it. To have yours taken down sooner, email hello@catholicdoorway.com with its wording.

A prayer request can reveal something about your health, faith or family. Please share only what you are comfortable having others read. We use requests only to pray and to run the Prayer Wall, never for anything else.

Email

When you write to hello@catholicdoorway.com, Cloudflare Email Routing forwards your message to our inbox, which is hosted by Google (Gmail). We use your address and message only to reply and to act on what you asked, such as a correction. We keep messages only as long as they are useful, and we will delete yours if you ask.

Information collected automatically

  • Basic request data. Like every website, when your browser asks for a page, our host Cloudflare receives your IP address, browser type, the page requested, the page that linked to it and the time. Cloudflare uses this to deliver the site and protect it from attacks and abuse, and may give us anonymous, overall traffic counts. We do not use it to identify you. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.
  • Spam protection on the form. To limit repeated submissions, the OCIA form keeps a scrambled code made from your IP address and the current hour for one hour. The code cannot be turned back into your IP address.
  • Spam protection on the Prayer Wall. To limit repeated requests, the Prayer Wall keeps a scrambled code made from your IP address and the current hour for one hour. When you tap I prayed, a scrambled code made from your IP address, the request and the date is kept for one day, so each visitor counts once a day. Neither code can be turned back into your IP address.
  • Cookies. We set no cookies. Cloudflare may set a strictly necessary security cookie to tell people from automated bots.
  • Visitor statistics. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits, for example how many people read a page and which country or type of device they use. It sets no cookies, does not store your IP address, does not follow you to other websites and does not build a profile of you. We see only totals. See Cloudflare Web Analytics.
  • Settings saved in your browser. The site remembers a few things on your own device using your browser’s local storage: light or dark theme, your language, whether you prefer Latin prayers, and your place in the Rosary, the Stations of the Cross or the First Mass companion, and which Prayer Wall requests you have tapped I prayed for. This stays on your device and is never sent to us. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

Your location on the church map

If you tap Near me on Find a Church, your browser asks your permission first. Your approximate location is then used on your device to pick nearby churches from our list. If our list does not cover your area, the map sends the coordinates of the area to OpenStreetMap’s Overpass service to look up churches there. If you type a city or ZIP code instead, that text is sent to OpenStreetMap’s Nominatim search to find the place. We never receive or store your location.

Services that load in your browser

A few outside services deliver parts of the site directly to your browser, so they receive your IP address and basic browser information under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces on every page (Google privacy policy).
  • OpenStreetMap supplies the map images, place search and live church lookups on the church map only (OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy).

Calendar feeds. If you subscribe to our prayer reminders or holy days calendar, your calendar app downloads the feed from our server from time to time. The feed’s web address holds only the reminders you picked and your language, nothing that identifies you. Your calendar provider (for example Google, Apple or Microsoft) handles the subscription under its own policy.

Links. We link to many other websites, such as the USCCB for the daily readings and the Vatican for Church documents. Once you follow a link, that site’s own privacy policy applies.

How we share information

We share personal information only in these ways:

  • Approved OCIA listings are published on this site, as described above.
  • The companies that run the site for us handle it as part of that service: Cloudflare (hosting, storage, email forwarding) and Google (our email inbox and fonts).
  • If the law requires it, for example in answer to a valid court order.

We never sell or rent personal information, and we never share it for advertising.

Do Not Track

Some browsers can send a “Do Not Track” or Global Privacy Control signal. We do not track visitors over time or across other websites, so the site works the same way whether or not the signal is on. There is nothing to switch off.

Children

CatholicDoorway is written for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. The OCIA form is meant for parish staff and volunteers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Keeping information safe

The whole site uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and the page where we review OCIA listings is protected by a password. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep as little personal information as we can, which is the best protection of all.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete any personal information you have sent us, including a published OCIA listing. Email hello@catholicdoorway.com and we will answer within 30 days. Some states, such as California, give their residents specific privacy rights; we honor these requests for everyone, wherever you live.

Changes to this policy

If the site starts handling information in a new way, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect. If a change is significant, we will say so clearly here.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hello@catholicdoorway.com. See also our Terms of Use.
