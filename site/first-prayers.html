First Prayers for Kids

The first prayers every Catholic child learns, in big letters, with a button that reads each one out loud so little ones can pray along.

  • The Sign of the Cross
  • The Our Father
  • The Hail Mary
  • Glory Be
  • Prayer to Your Guardian Angel
  • Grace Before Meals
  • Morning Prayer
  • Bedtime Prayer
  • Act of Contrition
Reading speed: Your device has no voice for this language, so read-aloud is turned off.

The Sign of the Cross

In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen.

Touch your forehead (Father), your chest (Son), your left shoulder and then your right shoulder (Holy Spirit).

The Our Father

Our Father, who art in heaven,hallowed be thy name;thy kingdom come,thy will be done on earth as it is in heaven.Give us this day our daily bread,and forgive us our trespasses,as we forgive those who trespass against us;and lead us not into temptation,but deliver us from evil. Amen.

Jesus taught His friends this prayer. We call God “Father” because He loves us as His children.

The Hail Mary

Hail Mary, full of grace, the Lord is with thee.Blessed art thou among women,and blessed is the fruit of thy womb, Jesus.Holy Mary, Mother of God,pray for us sinners,now and at the hour of our death. Amen.

The first words are what the angel Gabriel said to Mary. We ask Mary, Jesus’ mother, to pray for us.

Glory Be

Glory be to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit,as it was in the beginning, is now, and ever shall be, world without end. Amen.

A short prayer of praise to God the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit. Bow your head while you say the first line.

Prayer to Your Guardian Angel

Angel of God, my guardian dear,to whom God’s love commits me here,ever this day be at my side,to light and guard, to rule and guide. Amen.

God gives each of us an angel to watch over us. Say this prayer in the morning and at night.

Grace Before Meals

Bless us, O Lord, and these thy gifts,which we are about to receive from thy bounty,through Christ our Lord. Amen.

Say it before every meal, holding hands or making the Sign of the Cross together.

Morning Prayer

Dear God, thank you for this new day.Help me to be kind,to tell the truth and to do my best.Be with my family and my friends.Amen.

Say it when you wake up, before you get out of bed.

Bedtime Prayer

Dear Jesus, thank you for today.I am sorry for the times I was not kind.Please bless my family and keep us safe tonight.Mary, my mother, pray for me.Amen.

Before this prayer, think about your day: one thing you are thankful for and one thing you are sorry for.

Act of Contrition

O my God, I am heartily sorry for having offended thee,and I detest all my sins because of thy just punishments,but most of all because they offend thee, my God,who art all good and deserving of all my love.I firmly resolve, with the help of thy grace,to sin no more and to avoid the near occasion of sin. Amen.

Children learn this prayer to say in their First Confession. It tells God we are sorry and want to do better.

Teaching prayers to your child

  • Pray one line and let your child repeat it. In a few weeks they will know it by heart.
  • Pick a regular time: before meals, in the car, or at bedtime.
  • Use your own words too. Teach your child that they can talk to God about anything.
  • Do not worry about perfect words. God hears the heart.
