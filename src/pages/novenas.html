Novenas

A novena is nine days of prayer for a special intention, usually leading up to a feast. The first novena was the nine days Mary and the apostles prayed together between the Ascension and Pentecost.

How to pray a novena

  1. Choose an intention. Something you need, someone you love, or simply to grow closer to God.
  2. Pray each day for nine days in a row. Pray the novena prayer below, then an Our Father, a Hail Mary and a Glory Be. Five minutes is enough.
  3. If you miss a day, just pick up where you left off. A novena is a habit of trust, not a test.
