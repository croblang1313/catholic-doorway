About this list

The steps on the road to sainthood are explained on our Saints page. This directory comes from Wikidata, a free database kept up by volunteers around the world. It is refreshed every week, so new beatifications and canonizations appear here soon after volunteers record the Vatican’s decrees. Official announcements come from the Dicastery for the Causes of Saints.

Wikidata is very good but not perfect. Some people are missing, and some open causes are not yet recorded. Each entry links to its Wikidata page, where anyone can make a correction.
