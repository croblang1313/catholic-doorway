New to the Catholic Church?

You are welcome at any Catholic Mass, no matter what you believe. You don’t need to sign up, dress up or know what to do. Sit toward the back, watch, and listen.

When you’re ready to learn more, the Church has a path for adults called OCIA (the Order of Christian Initiation of Adults, formerly RCIA). It starts with a simple phone call to a parish.

How to get started

Coming back to the faith?

Many Catholics step away for a while. Coming home is simpler than you might think. Any priest can hear your Confession, and most parishes list Confession times on their website.

If you were baptized Catholic but never confirmed, ask your parish about adult Confirmation.
