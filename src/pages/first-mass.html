  • Stand

  • As you wish

Customs vary a little from parish to parish. When in doubt, follow the people around you. If kneeling is hard for you, sitting is always fine. The words people say come from the Roman Missal; the full texts are in the missal or worship aid in the pew.

A few more things to know

  • How long is Mass? About an hour on Sundays, and about 30 minutes on weekdays.
  • What should I wear? Something you’d wear to a nice dinner is typical, but come as you are.
  • Can I bring children? Yes. Many churches have a cry room, but most parishes are happy to hear little ones.
  • Do I have to give money? No. The collection supports the parish. Visitors are welcome to let the basket pass.
  • Where do I find Mass times? Use Find a Church, then check the parish website or call.
