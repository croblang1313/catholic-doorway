The Stations of the Cross

Walk with Jesus to Calvary, one station at a time. Each of the fourteen stations has a picture, a short Scripture passage, a reflection and the traditional prayers. Use it at home, or on your phone as you walk from station to station in church. It takes about 20 minutes, and your place is saved if you stop.

When: any time, but especially on the Fridays of Lent and on Good Friday, when most parishes pray them together, often on Friday evening. Get a reminder on Fridays in Lent.
