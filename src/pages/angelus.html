The Angelus

A one-minute prayer that remembers the moment God became man. Catholics pray it three times a day, at 6 a.m., noon and 6 p.m., and church bells have rung for it for centuries. During the Easter season it is replaced by the joyful Regina Caeli.

How to pray it

One person (or you, if you pray alone) says the lines marked V., and everyone answers with the lines marked R. A Hail Mary follows each of the first three verses. Many people pause wherever they are when the bells ring or the clock strikes, even in the middle of work. The Pope leads the Angelus from his window in St. Peter’s Square every Sunday at noon.
