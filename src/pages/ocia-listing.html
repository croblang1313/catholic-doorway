List your parish’s OCIA

People exploring the Catholic faith often do not know who to call. If you coordinate or help with OCIA at a U.S. parish, add your contact here and we will show it to people searching near you. It is free and takes three minutes. You can also add your Mass and Confession times, which we show with your church on the map.

What happens next

  1. You fill in the form below.
  2. We check it with your parish, usually within a week, before anything is published.
  3. Your listing appears on this page and helps people find you from our Begin Here guide and church map.

To change or remove a listing at any time, email hello@catholicdoorway.com.

Looking for OCIA yourself?

Browse the parishes that have listed their OCIA below, or find any parish near you on the map and call the office. Every parish can help you get started.
