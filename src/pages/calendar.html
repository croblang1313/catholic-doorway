What these mean

  • Holy Day of Obligation Catholics attend Mass, as on a Sunday. An evening Mass the day before also counts.
  • Fast and abstinence Catholics ages 18 to 59 eat one full meal and two smaller meals that together don’t equal a full meal; everyone 14 and older does not eat meat.
  • Abstinence Catholics 14 and older don’t eat meat (fish is allowed) on the Fridays of Lent.
  • Solemnity The highest rank of feast. Feast Important days in the Church’s year.

Anyone who is ill, pregnant, or for whom fasting would harm their health is excused. A few U.S. provinces (Boston, Hartford, New York, Newark, Omaha and Philadelphia) keep the Ascension on Thursday rather than Sunday. When in doubt, check your parish bulletin or diocese.
